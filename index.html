Who needs the new bins?
Every building with 1-9 residential units - houses through nine-unit buildings. About 750,000 buildings citywide.

Your property

What rules apply to me?

Pick the property type. You get the rule, the deadline, the enforcement status, and what you actually need to do.

Residential, 1-9 units

Houses, brownstones, and buildings up to nine apartments - plus houses of worship, nonprofits, and professional offices in residential buildings on DSNY collection.
The rule
Trash must be set out in official NYC Bins only. Mandatory since June 1, 2026.
Enforcement
Live since Sept 8, 2026. $50 first offense, $100 second, $200 third and beyond.
Recycling & compost
No official bin required. Any labeled bin (55 gal max), or clear bags for recycling.
Who's responsible
The property owner or manager - for buying the bins and for the fines.
What you actually need to do
Count your weekly trash, buy enough official NYC Bins to hold it (25/35/45 gal, $45-$55 each), and set them out after 6 PM. How to actually get a bin →
Sources: DSNY Residential Containerization · DSNY PR #26-035

Residential, 10+ units

Rules depend on your district. Check yours before buying bins.
Manhattan CD9
West Harlem's 31+ unit buildings already use assigned on-street Empire Bins.
Brooklyn CD2
Buildings with 31+ units must use assigned Empire Bins by October 15, 2026.
10-30 units in both districts
Buildings that did not opt in to Empire Bins must use official NYC Bins for trash by October 15, 2026.
Elsewhere
DSNY is rolling out larger-building containerization district by district, targeting citywide completion by June 2032.
What you actually need to do
Check whether your building is in Manhattan CD9 or Brooklyn CD2. If it has 10-30 units and no assigned Empire Bin, get enough official NYC Bins for all its trash. In another district, check your DSNY notice before buying bins. See the timeline →
Sources: DSNY Brooklyn CD2 guide · Adopted rule for 10+ unit buildings

Commercial / business

Every NYC business that sets out waste at the curb. (Businesses with loading-dock collection are exempt.)
The rule
Since March 1, 2024, all trash, food and food-soiled paper must be in a bin with a secure lid. No bags on the curb.
Official NYC Bin?
Not required. Any rigid bin with a tight-fitting lid qualifies.
Capacity
Enough bins to hold 72 hours of waste.
Set-out
1 hour before closing, or after 8:00 PM.
What you actually need to do
Make sure your business has enough lidded bins for three days of waste, and that whoever closes puts them out at the right time. Any good lidded bin works.
Source: DSNY Commercial Setout & Containers

The practical part

How to actually get an official NYC Bin

NYC publishes the rule. We follow it to your front door: which size, how many, where they're in stock, and how to get one home without a car.

  1. Pick a size

    45 gal holds about 4 kitchen bags ($54.60), 35 gal about 3 ($47.25), 25 gal about 2 ($45.19). Buy enough to hold everything between collections - there's no limit on bins.

  2. Buy it

    Every Home Depot in the five boroughs stocks them. Order ahead for in-store pickup at homedepot.com/NYCBins.

  3. No car? Get it delivered

    Order from your local Home Depot through DoorDash, Instacart, or Uber Eats for same-day home delivery.

  4. Label it

    Every NYC Bin looks identical. Mark yours with your address before it goes out - see below.
